## Overview

Two camera configurations have been used across collection seasons. The majority of images use the [Sony Alpha 7R IV](#sony-alpha-7r-iv), while more recent acquisitions use the [SVS-Vistek SHR 10GigE (shr661CXGE)](#svs-vistek-shr-10gige-shr661cxge).

Images are captured with at least 33% overlap from both front and side perspectives. A color calibration checkerboard is photographed at the start of each collection day to ensure consistent color reproduction.

Used for the majority of images. Mounted on the BenchBot gantry with interchangeable lenses (55 mm or 35 mm) and a Godox AR400 ring flash.
